Walk in to Central Outreach Hub

Central Outreach Hub is a peer-led space in downtown Calgary at 131 7th Avenue SW. People drop in to warm up, eat, rest, and talk with peers. The space is open Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m., and you do not need to call ahead.

Lower level of Central United Church, 131 7 Avenue SW  |  Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift

Call 403-606-7892Hours and directions

How it works

You can drop in any time the room is open. You do not need an appointment, a referral or ID, and you can stay for the time you have. A peer at the door will greet you and help you find what you need.

People drop in for different reasons. Some come for the coffee and a warm seat. Some come for the hot meal served on both days. Some come to talk with a peer about something they are working through. All of that is what the room is for.

Where to find us

The space is at 131 7th Avenue SW, on the 7th Avenue free fare zone for C-Train. The Hub is in the lower level of Central United Church. Use the door on 1 Street SW beside the lift.

Central Outreach Hub and the Calgary Drop-In Centre are different organizations. If you were looking for the Drop-In Centre at 1 Dermot Baldwin Way SE, that is a different organization a few blocks away.

Address and hours

131 7th Avenue SW, Calgary, AB. Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift. Open Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m. A hot meal is served on both days. See full visit details.

If you are coming in with someone

Frontline workers, family members, and friends are welcome to come in alongside the person they are walking with. There is no referral to send in advance, and no form to fill out at the door.
