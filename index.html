Services

What you can find at the Hub

Some of this is run by COH peers and volunteers. Some is offered by visiting organizations on their own schedules. Call 403-606-7892 to check what is on before you travel.

Basic Needs & Stabilization

Run by COH peers, volunteers and community teams.

A hot meal on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Clothing and hygiene supplies when available.

Peer Support & Engagement

Run by COH.

Peers with lived experience are in the room on every service day. Talk, or just sit. Both are fine.

Service Connection

Partner organizations operate within the Hub and provide direct connection to housing, income support, identification, and healthcare. COH provides the coordinated environment where these connections are initiated and sustained.

Indigenous Wellness

Indigenous communities carry their own cultural programming, ceremony, and gathering. COH provides the space where that work happens.

Health Access

Visiting organizations.

Foot care, wound care, community nursing and testing for sexually transmitted and blood-borne infections are offered at the Hub on varying days. You can walk in. Call 403-606-7892 to ask when a service is next available.

Housing Stability Support

In development.

COH is piloting peer support for people moving into housing, alongside the housing worker who holds their file. Results will be published once the pilot has a full cohort.

About

A shared room

Central Outreach Hub is a day space in the lower level of Central United Church in downtown Calgary. People come in to get warm, eat, rest and talk with peers who have lived through homelessness and recovery themselves.

Peers and volunteers run the room: the welcome at the door, the clothing, the conversation and the Tuesday meal. Other organizations visit to offer their own services in the same space, such as health care, identification and housing help. Each organization runs and answers for its own service.

Because people already know the peers, an introduction to a nurse or a housing worker can happen on the spot, when the person is ready.

Our Values

  • Low-barrier, no prerequisites, no wrong door

How It Works

How the room works

COH runs the room and the peer shift. Visiting organizations bring their services to it. Here is how the three parts fit.

01

Walk-in access.

One room where anyone can come in during open hours. Entry does not require an appointment, a referral or ID. Some visiting services have their own requirements, and a peer can tell you what to bring.

02

Visiting organizations.

Organizations offer their own services in the Hub on their own schedules. COH schedules the space and sets shared expectations for everyone working in the room.

03

Peers on every shift.

Experienced peers lead each shift, welcome people and introduce them to visiting workers when they want that.
