Finding the door

The Hub is in the lower level of Central United Church, across 7 Avenue from the 1 Street SW C-Train platform. Go around the corner onto 1 Street SW. The door is beside the lift, and a peer will greet you. If you need the lift, call 403-606-7892 before you travel to check that it is working.

You do not need an appointment, a referral or ID to come in, and you can come back as often as you like. Full directions and what to expect on a first visit.

A shared room, run by peers

Central Outreach Hub is a day space in the lower level of Central United Church in downtown Calgary. People come in to get warm, eat, rest and talk with peers who have lived through homelessness and recovery themselves.

Peers and volunteers run the room: the welcome at the door, the clothing, the conversation and the Tuesday meal. Other organizations visit to offer their own services in the same space, such as health care, identification and housing help. Each organization runs and answers for its own service.

Because people already know the peers, an introduction to a nurse or a housing worker can happen on the spot, when the person is ready.

How the room works

Walk-in access. One room where anyone can come in during open hours. Entry does not require an appointment, a referral or ID. Some visiting services have their own requirements, and a peer can tell you what to bring.

Visiting organizations. Organizations offer their own services in the Hub on their own schedules. The Hub schedules the space and sets shared expectations for everyone working in the room.

Peers on every shift. Experienced peers lead each shift, welcome people and introduce them to visiting workers when they want that.

Our values

  • Peer-led engagement and governance
  • Low-barrier, no prerequisites, no wrong door
  • Multiple pathways to stability and recovery
  • Dignity, choice, and self-determination
  • Coordinated partner organizations, unified framework
  • Culturally grounded, Indigenous-informed

Activity to date

The Hub sees about 290 visits on each service day, averaged across 60 recorded service dates. Someone who leaves and comes back is counted each time.

From February 3 to September 1, 2026, peers recorded about 17,600 visits at the door across those 60 service dates. The total includes repeat entries and one estimated daily count.

Volunteers give an estimated 60 hours on each service day, based on counts from ten service days in February and March 2026. The Hub is open two days every week, year-round.

These figures count visits and hours. They do not yet show what changed for people.

Who is accountable

Central Outreach Hub is a program of Central United Church, a registered charity (118844042RR0001). The church is the legal and financial host: it holds the funds, is accountable for grant agreements and issues tax receipts. Brad Pert and Tamara Llewellyn co-lead the Hub's daily operation. Brad leads funding, reporting and administration. Tamara leads the floor, peer shifts and community relationships. Visiting organizations remain responsible for their own services.
