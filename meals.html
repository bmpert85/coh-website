Meals in downtown Calgary

The kitchen at Central Outreach Hub serves a hot community meal on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 131 7th Avenue SW. Walk in during Hub hours and you will be welcome at the table.

Lower level of Central United Church, 131 7 Avenue SW  |  Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift

Community meals: Tuesdays and Thursdays

Community meals happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 131 7th Avenue SW. Tuesdays the room is open 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m. There is no charge, no ticket, and no sign-up needed. If you can come, come.

The kitchen is held by the people who use the space, the peers who work in it, and the volunteers who come in week after week. We keep the room open and the kitchen warm, and the community holds the work that happens inside it. The meal is not a program we run for visitors. It is a regular gathering that people show up to and take part in, and we try to make sure the room is ready when they arrive.

Saturday meal and clothing

A separately run meal takes place in the building on Saturdays, 2:00 to 5:00 p.m., with clothing available. COH provides the space. The Saturday meal is run independently of COH.

Other gatherings in the space

Beyond the regular community meals, the room sometimes hosts smaller gatherings during the week. Call 403-606-7892 to ask about other gatherings and who they are for.
