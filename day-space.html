A day space in downtown Calgary

Central Outreach Hub is a peer-led space at 131 7th Avenue SW where people come in during the day to be warm, eat something, rest, and talk with peers who have been through the same systems.

Lower level of Central United Church, 131 7 Avenue SW  |  Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift

Call 403-606-7892Hours and directions

What we mean by a day space

You can walk into the Hub during opening hours without an appointment or referral. The room is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and you can come in and stay for the time you have. You do not need an appointment, a referral or ID to come in.

People come in for different reasons. Some need a warm place to be. Some are between appointments and want somewhere to wait that is not a hallway. Some come for the conversation, and some come because they would rather not be alone for a stretch. All of that is fine.

What you can usually expect

When you walk in, a peer will say hello. You can take any open seat. Coffee is on most of the time, and the kitchen serves a hot meal on both days. If you want to talk through something you are working on, someone is there to listen. If you would rather just sit quietly, that works too.

People also drop in here on the way to or from other appointments, and that is part of what the room is for. If you are searching for a place you can walk into without an appointment, this is one of them.

Address and hours

131 7th Avenue SW, Calgary, AB. Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift. Open Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m. A hot meal is served on both days. See full visit details.

If you are coming from outreach

Frontline workers, nurses, and family members are welcome to come in with the person they are walking alongside. You do not need to send a referral form or call ahead. If you would like to give us a heads up, the phone number is on the visit page.
