Central Outreach Hub is a peer-led space in downtown Calgary where people come in to be warm, eat, rest, and talk with peers who have been through the same systems. You can walk in during operating hours without an appointment or a referral.

Address

131 7th Avenue SW, Calgary, AB. The entrance is on 7th Avenue SW, and the room is on the lower level.

Hours

The Hub is open two days a week, every week: Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m.

A hot meal is served on both days. Around holidays, call (403) 606-7892 before you travel to confirm opening hours.

Saturday meal and clothing

A separately run meal takes place in the building on Saturdays, 2:00 to 5:00 p.m., with clothing available. COH provides the space. The Saturday meal is run independently of COH.

Getting here

The 7th Avenue free fare zone for C-Train runs right past the front door, so most downtown stops are a short walk. The closest stops are 4 Street SW and 1 Street SW. From either of those, walk along 7th Avenue and you will find the entrance at 131.

If you are coming on foot from another part of downtown, the building sits between 1 Street SW and Centre Street, on the south side of 7th Avenue. The room is on the lower level. Call (403) 606-7892 to ask about access before you travel.

What to expect on a first visit

When you walk in, a peer will say hello and ask if there is anything they can help you with. You can take any open seat in the room. Coffee is usually on, and a hot meal is served on Tuesdays and Thursdays. If you would like to talk through what you are working on, someone is there to listen. If you would rather just sit in a warm room without anyone bothering you, that works too.

You do not need ID, an appointment or a reason to come in. Some visiting services, such as identification clinics, have their own requirements. A peer can tell you what to bring.

For helpers and frontline workers

If you are bringing someone in from outreach, the same applies. You do not need to call ahead or send a referral form. Walk in together and a peer will take it from there. If you would like to give us a heads up so the room can be ready, the phone number is below.
