A warm space in downtown Calgary

If you need a warm place to be during the day, Central Outreach Hub is open Tuesdays and Thursdays at 131 7th Avenue SW. The room is warm, there are seats and coffee, and the door does not require an appointment to walk through.

Lower level of Central United Church, 131 7 Avenue SW  |  Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift

Call 403-606-7892Hours and directions

What you can find here

The room is warm and has seats during Hub hours: Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m. Coffee is usually on, and there is a peer at the door who will greet you when you arrive. If you want to talk, someone is around to talk with. If you would rather just thaw out for a while without being asked anything, that is fine too.

A hot meal is served on both days, so you can warm up and eat in the same stop.

Who comes in

People come in for different reasons. Some are spending the day downtown and need a place to sit between things. Some are coming in from the weather. Some are looking for somewhere to be that is not loud or rushed. All of that is what the room is for.

Address

131 7th Avenue SW, Calgary, AB. Door on 1 Street SW beside the lift. Open Tuesdays 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and Thursdays 12:00 to 2:00 p.m. See full visit details.

If you are helping someone find a warm place

Frontline workers, family members, and friends are welcome to walk in alongside someone. There is no referral to send and no form to fill out before arriving. If you would like to call ahead so the room is ready, the phone number is on the visit page.
