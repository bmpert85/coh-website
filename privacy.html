Privacy

What this website collects, how photos are used, and who to contact.

What this website collects

This website does not ask for your name, your email or any other personal information. It has no forms, no accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies. We do not use analytics tools to follow what you do on the site.

If you email or call us, we use your contact details only to reply to you.

Other services this website uses

A few outside services work alongside this site. Each has its own privacy policy.

Donations

Donations to the Hub are made through Central United Church. The church receives your donation, keeps the donor information it needs and issues tax receipts. This website does not handle payments or card details. Questions about a donation go to donations@centralchurch.ca.

Visiting the Hub

You do not need to show ID or have an appointment or a referral to come in. We count the number of visits at the door so we can report how much the Hub is used.

Organizations that offer services at the Hub keep their own records and follow their own privacy practices. Ask them directly how they handle your information.

Photos

We ask for written permission before using a photo of an identifiable person. Permission is for a specific use, and you can withdraw it at any time.

To have a photo removed, tell any peer at the Hub or email hey@centraloutreachhub.ca.
